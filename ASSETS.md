# Vehicle image provenance

`public/transport-vehicles.png` was generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this presentation. The four quadrants depict a sedan, MPV, hatchback and SUV; they are illustrative pictures, not photographs of an actual provider's fleet.

Prompt: One clean 2x2 photographic contact sheet, four equal panels without gutters or text, each a generic Indian taxi in front three-quarter view on warm beige ground: white sedan, cream MPV, silver hatchback, forest green SUV. Whole vehicles, consistent camera and scale, natural daylight, no people or real business identification.

The supplied LAABH logo is used unchanged. Blue verification badges denote the existing presentation verification state, not government verification of real businesses.
